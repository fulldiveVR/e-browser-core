Connect to Your Parent Account

To start browsing safely, you need to connect this device to your parent's Education-OS account.

How to Connect:

  1. 1 Ask your parent to visit learning.aiwayz.com
  2. 2 Get your 6-digit pairing code from them
  3. 3 Open a new tab in your browser to complete the pairing process
  4. 4 Once paired, you can start browsing and chatting with your AI assistant!
